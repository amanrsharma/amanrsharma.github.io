Hello! I received PhD from the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering, IIT Delhi, in 2026. There I worked under the supervision of Prof. Nezamuddin. My work addressed energy supply uncertainties in the design and charging scheduling of electric bus systems.

Before this, I did my M.Tech at the Department of Civil Engineering, NIT Hamirpur, under the supervision of Prof. Raman Parti. During this time, I worked on the design of fast transit bus corridors for a city.

I like working on real-world transportation systems, aiming to create reliable, resilient systems that operate effectively under uncertainty and disruptions. My research interests include planning and optimizing large-scale transportation systems and their operations, integrating clean energy, sequential decision-making, and addressing uncertainties to ensure resilient, reliable operations using operations research techniques.

Apart from this, I enjoy generic things like playing video games, tinkering with Android and Linux, listening to music, consuming media, and exploring places.
